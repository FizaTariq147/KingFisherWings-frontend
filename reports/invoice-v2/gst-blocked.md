# GST split blocked

These codes have SGST, CGST, or IGST columns. They render with `taxMode: single`. The split headings and amounts are not drawn, and the other columns stay where they are. No SGST, CGST, or IGST amount is invented from the single tax total.

Blocked until the backend stores the SGST/CGST/IGST breakdown.
